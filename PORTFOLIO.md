# Business Analytics: Statistical Analysis
## Selected Coursework and Applications

This portfolio presents recovered statistics and business-analytics work focused on probability, sampling distributions, confidence intervals, and sample-size determination. It is intentionally limited to material that could be verified from the surviving coursework workbook.

## Portfolio Contents

### 1. Probability & Normal Distributions
Worked applications of normal-distribution probabilities and percentile calculations, including individual observations.

### 2. Sampling Distributions
Analysis of sample means using standard error and normal probability methods.

### 3. Binomial Probability
Discrete probability calculations for exact, cumulative, and upper-tail outcomes.

### 4. Confidence Intervals for Means
Construction of confidence intervals using both t and z methods, depending on whether the population standard deviation is known.

### 5. Confidence Intervals for Proportions
Estimation of population proportions using sample data, standard error, critical values, and margin of error.

### 6. Sample Size Determination
Calculation of required sample sizes for estimating means and proportions at specified confidence levels and margins of error.
